how searching works
  • Searches look for the text you type, ignoring capitals and punctuation. brendan also finds "Brendan's", and dont finds "don't".
  • Small typos are forgiven. If a word looks misspelt, similar spellings show up under close matches, e.g. oscer finds "Oscar".
  • Join up to 10 topics with + to find episodes that mention all of them, e.g. epstein + trump.
  • Tick whole words only so ian doesn't match "villian". You can also use ~ as a forced space: ~ian~.
  • Click a timestamp to read that moment in the full transcript.
  • Hit ☆ save on any result or transcript line to keep it in your saved tab.
